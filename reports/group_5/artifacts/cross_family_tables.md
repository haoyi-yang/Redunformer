## Duplicate-pair ablation (H5)

`1st twin` is the cost of masking one neuron of each near-duplicate pair; `2nd twin` is the extra cost of then masking its partner. `2nd > 1st` is the direct evidence that the survivor was carrying the load. `control` is the matched-importance arm H5 is scored against, and `coverage` is how much of the duplicates' importance that control actually reached - well below 1.0 means the control is not really matched and the verdict should not be read as a refutation.
